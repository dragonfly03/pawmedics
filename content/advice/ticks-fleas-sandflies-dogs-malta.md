Malta's warm climate is lovely for people, and for parasites too. Fleas and ticks are active for much of the year, and in the warmer months sandflies spread **leishmaniasis**, one of the most serious diseases we see in dogs. Regular protection works very well.

## Sandflies and leishmaniasis

**What it is.** Leishmaniasis is caused by a tiny parasite, *Leishmania infantum*, passed on by the bite of an infected sandfly. In Malta the main carrier is a sandfly called *Phlebotomus perniciosus*. Dogs are the main animals affected, and the disease has been present in Malta for over a century.

**Why it matters.** Some infected dogs stay well for months or years, while others become seriously ill. Leishmaniasis can affect the skin, eyes, joints and kidneys, and it needs long-term treatment. It can also affect people, which is another reason to protect your dog.

**Signs to watch for:**

- hair loss, especially around the eyes, ears and nose, and scaly, flaky skin
- sores that don't heal, often on the ears, nose or elbows
- overgrown or brittle claws
- weight loss and tiredness
- nosebleeds
- drinking and peeing more, which can be a sign of kidney damage

If you notice any of these, book a check-up and mention leishmaniasis.

### How to protect your dog from sandflies

The best protection uses more than one method:

1. **A repellent product throughout the sandfly season.** Repellent collars and some spot-on treatments stop most sandflies biting. Fit the collar or apply the spot-on a few days before the season starts, and keep it going until the season ends. Sandflies are active in the warmer months, roughly from spring until late autumn.
2. **Keep your dog indoors from dusk to dawn** in the warmer months. Sandflies bite mostly in the evening and at night, and are common around rubble walls, gardens and farms.
3. **Use fly screens** on windows if your dog sleeps near them, and avoid walks at dusk near fields and rubble walls.
4. **Ask us about vaccination.** A vaccine against leishmaniasis is available for dogs from six months of age. It doesn't replace a repellent but adds extra protection. We'll test your dog before vaccinating.
5. **Screen every year.** A simple blood test before each sandfly season can find infection early, when treatment works best.

## Ticks

Ticks live in long grass, scrub and rubble walls, and are active for much of the year in Malta. They can pass on diseases such as **ehrlichiosis** and **babesiosis**, which cause fever, tiredness, bleeding and anaemia.

- Check your dog every day after walks, especially around the ears, face, neck, armpits and between the toes.
- Use a tick treatment all year round, as a tablet, spot-on or collar. Some products also repel sandflies.
- Remove ticks quickly with a tick hook, twisting gently. Don't squeeze, burn or cover the tick with oil.
- If your dog seems tired, has a fever, pale gums or nosebleeds after having ticks, call us.

## Fleas

Fleas are common all year in Malta's mild climate, and thrive in warm homes. They cause itching and skin allergies, and they can pass on tapeworm.

- Treat every dog and cat in the house regularly. Treating only one pet rarely works.
- Most of the flea population lives in your home as eggs and larvae, not on your pet. Vacuum often, especially sofas, rugs and pet beds, and wash bedding at a high temperature.
- If you see fleas, use a household spray as well as treating your pets, and keep treating for at least three months to break the life cycle.

## Keep your cats safe

This is very important. **Many dog flea, tick and sandfly products contain permethrin** or similar ingredients. These are safe for dogs but can cause tremors, seizures and death in cats.

- Never use a dog product on a cat.
- After putting a spot-on on your dog, keep it apart from cats until the treatment has dried.
- If your cat sleeps against your dog or grooms it, ask us about a sandfly product that is safer in a mixed household.

Cats can also catch leishmaniasis, although it's less common. Ask us about protecting cats that live outdoors.

## Which product is right?

There are many products, and each covers a different mix of parasites. The right one depends on your dog's age, weight, health, lifestyle, whether it swims, and whether you have cats at home. Bring your dog in and we'll put together a plan that covers fleas, ticks, sandflies and worms.

## Common questions

### Is leishmaniasis common in Malta?

It's present in dogs across the Maltese Islands. Cases have fallen over the years, but the sandfly that spreads it still lives here, and infected dogs may look healthy for a long time. That's why we recommend protecting every dog during the sandfly season.

### When is sandfly season in Malta?

Sandflies are active in the warmer months, roughly from spring until late autumn, and bite mostly from dusk to dawn. Start your dog's repellent before the weather warms up and keep it going until the cooler weather returns.

### Can people catch leishmaniasis from their dog?

Not directly. Leishmaniasis is passed on by sandfly bites, not by touching or living with a dog. But infected dogs are a source of the parasite for sandflies, so protecting dogs also helps protect people.

### Is there a vaccine for leishmaniasis?

Yes, for dogs from six months old. It reduces the risk of your dog becoming ill but doesn't stop sandflies biting, so it should always be used together with a repellent collar or spot-on.
